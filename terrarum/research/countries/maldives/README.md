## Corrections made during checking

- A plain "Hukuru Miskiy" lookup returned a different mosque of the same name in Hithadhoo, Addu; the Malé one was found by name and ward.
- "Hulhumale Hukuru Miskiy" photos on Commons are a different, modern mosque; not used.
- Natural Earth gives Malé the same ISO code as Meemu atoll (MV-12); areas are keyed by name.
- Natural Earth shows 19 atolls and 2 cities; the heading first said 20.
- Cut as unsupported: "caught one fish at a time", "fishing still feeds the country", "Sri Lanka above all", "small, crowded island city", "foreign workers" (the census says foreign residents), "largest of the six coral stone mosques" (the project calls it the biggest coral stone building, attributed).
- Atoll counts disagree between sources (26 natural atolls in government material, 27 in the Library of Congress study), so the site says "a double chain of atolls" without a number.
- UNESCO: the tourism board says the cemetery joined the tentative list in 2013; a search snippet attributed to UNESCO says the Maldives replaced its Hukuru Miskiy entry with "Coral Stone Mosques of Maldives" in 2013. whc.unesco.org refused automated reads, so only the tourism board's wording is used.
